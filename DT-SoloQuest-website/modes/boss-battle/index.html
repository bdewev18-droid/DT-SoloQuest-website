Different Mechanics

The Boss Battle Protocol

Boss Battle is not Minion Rush. Bosses operate on distinct combat rules that fundamentally shift how you approach your dice decisions, card conservation, and defense.

01

The Boss Combat Die

Unlike minions or heroes, the boss does not roll 5 dice: automation rolls its combat die (currently bosses only have 1 combat die) in a single roll with no rerolls. The result immediately triggers the corresponding attack.

02

Massive HP & Endurance

While standard minions fall in 2 to 3 rounds, bosses are built for a real war of attrition with health pools ranging from 50 to over 60 HP. You must manage your resources and defenses carefully to go the distance.

03

The Boss Always Goes First

The moment you enter the arena, you are under siege. Bosses seize the initiative on turn one. You begin the battle on the back foot, needing to survive their opening onslaught before you can assemble your offense.

Legendary Bosses & Unique Styles

Every boss is crafted with its own personality, AI attack priority, and combat style. Here are the titans currently stalking the solo arena.

Floor 1 • Ancient Flame Dragon

Naxarus the Devourer

The draconic ruler of Floor 1. Naxarus rolls a single combat die each turn to unleash 6 devastating attack variants (deck burning, die confiscation and undefendable breaths), while shrugging off incoming hits with his draconic armor die.

⚔️ Attack Arsenal (1 Combat Die)

  • Face 1 — Swoop: Removes 1 random token from Naxarus, heals 4 HP and deals 3 undefendable damage.
  • Face 2 — Ember Spark: The hero places top 3 deck cards into the discard pile, then suffers 8 damage.
  • Face 3 — Gashing Bite: Rolls 4 dice and deals damage equal to the sum of the 2 highest rolled dice.
  • Face 4 — Hoarding: Confiscates 1 hero die for their next turn, then inflicts 9 damage.
  • Face 5 — Thundering Roar: The hero must discard 1 card of choice and suffers 8 undefendable damage.
  • Face 6 — Dragon's Might: Deals 10 damage and rolls 1 extra die; on a 5 or 6, Swoop is also triggered.

🛡️ Dragon Defense (1 Die)

Defense roll: Face 1 = prevents 1 damage. Faces 2 to 5 = prevents 3 damage. Face 6 = prevents 5 damage.

Combat Structure: Zero rerolls: exactly 1 combat die on offense and 1 die on defense. Fast, brutal, and without compromise.

Floor 2 • Primordial Serpent & Venom

Ourokhor the Serpent

An ancient serpentine horror slumbering in the marsh depths. Ourokhor is exclusively dedicated to poison: spreading toxins, advancing his venom gauge, and overwhelming heroes with a relentless cascade of crippling debuffs.

⚔️ Attack Arsenal (1 Combat Die)

  • White (Toxic Strike): 5 raw damage.
  • Orange (Corrosive Spit): 7 damage and inflicts 1 Poison.
  • Red (Noxious Vapor): 7 undefendable damage and inflicts 1 Delayed Poison.

🛡️ Scale Defense & Venom Gauge

Scale Defense (1 Die): White: prevents 3 damage and inflicts 1 Poison. Orange: prevents 4 damage and inflicts 2 Poison. Red: prevents 5 damage, inflicts 2 Poison, and grants Riposte to Ourokhor.

Venom Gauge (0-24) & 9 Hero Debuff Milestones: Every point of poison damage suffered by the hero advances the gauge (0-24). Reaching a milestone immediately afflicts the hero with a negative status token: 0 Targeted, 3 Knockdown, 6 Wither, 9 Prey, 12 Blind, 15 Bleed, 18 Blinding Light, 21 Hex, and 24 Stun (no passive heal).

Proposals are reviewed and playtested by our team to guarantee balanced math, exciting counter-play, and seamless integration with the automated dice engine. Boss submissions and balancing tools are built directly inside the Solo Quest app.
