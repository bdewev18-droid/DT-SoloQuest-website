06

Four structured combat phases

Every round follows a clear 4-phase flow built seamlessly into the battle bar at the top of the screen:

  1. Phase 1 — First Player Upkeep: Merges Upkeep, Income/Draw, and Main Phase into a single streamlined step. The app handles token triggers automatically.
  2. Phase 2 — First Player Roll Phase: The active player executes their attack while the opponent resolves defense.
  3. Phase 3 — Second Player Upkeep: Transitions cleanly to the second player's Upkeep, Draw, and Main actions.
  4. Phase 4 — Second Player Roll Phase: Executed by either the Hero or the Enemy Automation depending on initiative.
07

Smart AI dice & full player override

The automation chooses dice to reserve and reroll automatically based on each enemy's strategy. However, you always keep control: if the AI ever selects the wrong die to keep, tap Deselect to override the selection and keep the duel 100% fair.
